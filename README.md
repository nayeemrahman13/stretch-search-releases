# Doe

Doe explains what you're reading on your Mac. Highlight a word, name, or equation in any app, then click Doe (the croissant on your screen) or press **⇧⌘L** to get a clear explanation beside it, with sources. Ask a follow-up question when you want to go deeper.

[Download Doe](https://github.com/nayeemrahman13/stretch-search-releases/releases/latest/download/Doe.zip) · [Release notes](https://github.com/nayeemrahman13/stretch-search-releases/releases/latest) · [usedoe.tech](https://usedoe.tech)

Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac. Releases are signed and notarized.

Doe was previously called Stretch Search. Releases before 1.4.0 ship as `StretchSearch.zip`.

## Install

1. Download the ZIP and unzip it.
2. Move `Doe.app` to Applications and open it.
3. Follow setup to allow Accessibility, then highlight some text and click Doe or press **⇧⌘L**.

Doe lives in the menu bar. Click its icon to reopen your last explanation, open Settings, or check for updates. The included AI provider needs no API key.

## Updates

Version 1.1.0 and later can update inside the app. Choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar or open **Settings → Updates**. Daily checks default on; automatic downloads are optional. Stretch Search installs update to Doe in place and keep their settings, history, and permissions.

If you have a version older than 1.1.0, quit it and replace the app in Applications once. Future updates can be installed inside the app.

This repository contains app downloads and the signed automatic update feed.
